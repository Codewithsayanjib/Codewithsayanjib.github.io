Research Output

01
✓ Accepted COMSYS 2026
Evaluating SLMs on Understanding Structured Frameworks using Cluster Aware Prompting
Sur, S., and Bhaumik, P.
Proceedings of COMSYS 2026
▾
Designed a cluster-aware prompting pipeline partitioning 5 SLMs (1.5B–7B) into context-length groups (2K–4K, 8K, 16K+) with distinct zero-shot, one-shot, and self-augmented prompt families. Conducted 10,800 inference runs per model across 6 task types, 6 serialization formats (HTML, JSON, Markdown, Turtle, TXT, XML), and 5 questionnaire datasets using Ollama on Google Colab Pro (A100). Best overall accuracy: Vicuna-7B (35%); multi-hop inference collapsed below 6% across all models, exposing a hard reasoning ceiling in current SLMs.
02
⏳ Submitted IEEE ICCUBEA 2026
Structured Communication and Ensemble Diversity in SLM-based Multi-Agent Systems: A Comparative Study
Sur, S., Sil Sarma, A., and Singh, P. K.
IEEE International Conference on Computing, Communication, Control and Automation (ICCUBEA 2026)
▾
Designed two complementary multi-agent methodologies on the SVAMP benchmark across 5 SLMs (1.5B–7B): Pipeline Decomposition (sequential Planner → Solver → Verifier with 3 communication modes: natural, constrained, structured) and Diverse Ensemble Reasoning (parallel CoT, Formula-First, and Backward agents via majority vote). Best pipeline: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B (67%); Diverse Ensemble achieved LLaMA 3.2-3B at 81% accuracy (vs. 77% CoT baseline). Introduced NATE (Normalized Accuracy per Thousand Tokens) as an efficiency metric for fair cross-model comparison.
03
✍ In Preparation
Small Language Models: A Survey of Architecture, Scaling, Applications, and Deployment (2022–2026)
Sur, S., Ghosh, D., and Singh, P. K.
Manuscript in preparation
▾
A comprehensive survey covering the SLM landscape from 2022 to 2026 — tracing architectural evolution, scaling behaviour, domain-specific applications, and real-world deployment constraints. The survey synthesises findings from benchmarking studies, distillation techniques, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and agentic system design to provide a unified reference for researchers working in efficient NLP.
